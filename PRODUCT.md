# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, recruiters, and technical leads evaluating a front-end engineer. They arrive with limited time and a crowded inbox; they want to quickly confirm craft, clarity, and real-world delivery. Secondary audience: peers and potential collaborators who may refer or reach out.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio site that makes Manuel Mendez memorable for the right reasons: product thinking, clean execution, and communication. It should make a hiring manager feel confident that this candidate can ship useful software with humans in mind.

## Brand Personality

Confident minimal. Warm, precise, quietly human. Technical without being cold.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS/template portfolio: Awwwards-cliché hero, gradient text, hero-metric template, over-animated decoration.
- Cold corporate: navy + white, stock visuals, impersonal language, buzzwords.

## Design Principles

1. **Product before polish.** Every visual choice must support the story, not replace it.
2. **Clarity at every layer.** Navigation, copy, and hierarchy should be instantly legible.
3. **Show real work.** Projects, tools, and experience must feel grounded in actual delivery.
4. **Quiet confidence.** Use restraint and precision rather than loud effects to signal craft.
5. **Human warmth in technical context.** The site should feel approachable to non-technical visitors and credible to engineers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA baseline. Keyboard navigable, screen-reader friendly, reduced-motion respected, adequate contrast for dark theme.
